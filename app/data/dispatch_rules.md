# Dispatch Rules

## Instructor Rules
- Instructor must be rated for sortie type.
- Instructor must not exceed max duty hours per day.

## Aircraft Rules
- Aircraft under maintenance cannot be scheduled.
- Aircraft cannot be double booked.

## Student Rules
- Student cannot be double booked.
- Solo sorties allowed only for solo_eligible students.

## SIM Conversion Rule
If flight is NO_GO due to weather, convert to SIM if available.
If SIM not available, mark NEEDS_REVIEW.
